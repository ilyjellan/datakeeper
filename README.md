Before you install datakeeper, ensure that your system meets the following requirements:

- **Memory:** At least 4 GB of RAM

- **Graphics:** A 3D-capable graphics card

## 🔧 Features

datakeeper offers a variety of functions to help you manage your 3D data effectively:

- **User-Friendly Interface:** Navigate easily through various features.
- **Data Organization:** Store files in folders for better management.
- **3D Visualization Tools:** View and manipulate your 3D data directly.
- **Export Options:** Save your work in various formats for use in other applications.
- **Integration with Gemini:** Utilize gemini technology for enhanced performance and capabilities.

## 📝 Usage

Once you launch datakeeper, you will see the main dashboard. Here’s how to start:

1. **Create a New Project:** Click on "New Project" to begin organizing your files.
2. **Import Data:** Use the "Import" feature to bring your 3D files into the application.
3. **Manage Your Files:** Drag and drop files into folders to keep everything organized.
4. **Visualize Your Data:** Use the built-in tools to view and adjust your 3D models.
